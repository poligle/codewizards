Privacy policy

Last updated on 30 October 2026.

In short

CodeWizards collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and it never reads or stores your credentials.

The app

  • It runs on your computer and listens only on 127.0.0.1, behind a token in the room's address.
  • What it records stays on your computer, in its data folder (codewizards --help says where): each room's event log, your notes and the git worktrees of its quests. With --record it also keeps each turn's output, with secrets masked. Delete the folder and all of it is gone.
  • It sends nothing anywhere by itself and does not check for updates. The agent CLI it runs (Claude Code, today) sends your prompts and the code it reads to its maker, under your account and that maker's terms; see Anthropic's privacy policy. CodeWizards does not change what the CLI sends.
  • With --open-prs it pushes the quest's branch and opens a draft pull request, with your own git and gh, on the repository you chose.

This website

  • codewizards.app is a static site on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, no fonts and no scripts from anyone else.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser. It keeps its settings, such as the sound and where its scrolls are, in your browser's storage, and they never leave it.
  • GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address to run and protect the service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

npm and GitHub

Installing CodeWizards from npm or downloading it from GitHub follows their own policies.

Contact

For questions or requests, open an issue. Every change to this policy is in the repository's history.
